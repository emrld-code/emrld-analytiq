About us

Big-business capability, small-business scale

Emrld AnalytIQ is an Australian ICT and data company with a simple belief: the power of AI and automation shouldn't be reserved for companies with IT departments.

We work with SMEs to leverage data and AI agents — easing the pressure of every day activities that a handful of people (or one determined person) is doing. Our clients are not technical. They just need it explained properly, set up reliably, and supported by people who are.

Like the connected nodes in our logo, we believe the value is in the links — between your tools, your data and your day.

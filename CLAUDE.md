# Projects

To landing page website for my company. This website for provides information about my company which specialises in ICT & data.

The Company name is Emrld AnalytIQ


# How it works
- Content is managed in the primary project tracker
- Drafts are managed in the deliverables folder
- The landing page draft is `deliverables/landing-page-draft.html`
- The top banner nav has these items, in order: Our services, Pricing, Contact us, About us, Log in, Book a conversation
    - Our services, Pricing, Contact us, About us: scroll to their matching section on the page
    - Log in: aesthetic only, non-functional — no login flow exists
    - Book a conversation: primary CTA, links to the Contact us section
- The logo for Emrld AnalytIQ is in the folder: `Logo/`
- `Font/` holds the JetBrains Mono webfont files used site-wide (headings and body)
- `Refactor-images/` holds design-reference screenshots used to guide layout/style changes
- `error/` holds screenshots the user provides to flag bugs or regressions for review
